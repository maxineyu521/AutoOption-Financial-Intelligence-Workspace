Finalizer combines workflow status and answer mode.
  • complete | informational_only: the workflow finished and the final answer was intentionally kept read-only and non-actionable.
  • complete | directional_watchlist: the workflow finished with a directional or regime read, but still without a concrete live trade recommendation.
  • complete | actionable_options: the workflow finished with an options-specific actionable path, meaning the evidence and contract supported an options recommendation at structure or contract granularity.
  • degraded | ...: the workflow exited through a degraded fallback path rather than a normal complete render.

Per-case node columns are normalized node-influence scores, not raw metric values.

  • Analyst = semantic_evidence_carry_score: 0.0 means almost no required semantic evidence survived in the draft, 0.5 means partial carry, and 1.0 means strong carry across the requested slots.
  • Checker = fix_adoption_score: 0.0 means checker findings did not translate into cleaner final behavior, 0.5 means partial or mixed adoption, and 1.0 means clean adoption or no fix needed.
  • Critic = feedback_adoption_score: 0.0 means critic governance feedback did not land downstream, 0.5 means partial or mixed influence, and 1.0 means downgrade / risk / governance feedback was adopted cleanly.

These bars are all 0-1 normalized node-influence scores. They are not the same thing as the Value column in Node Contribution Analytics.

Structured mode is the final actionability mode chosen by the workflow:

  • informational_only: use this when the answer should stay read-only because the system lacks enough evidence or the governance layer forbids a live recommendation. When must_explain_why_not_now=True, the answer must explicitly explain why-not-now.
  • directional_watchlist: use this when a directional or regime view is supportable, but the evidence is still not strong enough for a concrete options trade. One clearly non-live illustrative structure hint is allowed when the contract permits it.
  • actionable_options: use this when the evidence and contract support a live options recommendation path with enough options-layer backing for structure-level or contract-level guidance.
  • Risk required: whether the governance contract said the final answer must explicitly carry risk language.
  • Risk retained: whether that required risk language actually survived into the final answer.
  • Illustrative applicable: whether the finalizer contract allowed an illustrative structure in this case.
  • Illustrative present: whether a legal non-live illustrative structure actually appeared in the final answer when it was allowed.
  • Compliance interpretation: informational_only remains stricter; directional_watchlist may include a monitor-only structure hint, but it fails if that hint crosses into active trade wording.
